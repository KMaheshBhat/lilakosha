### 6.4 Duplicates and Uniqueness

**100-row sample methodology:** For Part 1, I selected individual `section_url` values (e.g., `page-88`, `page-76`) and ran:

```sql
SELECT 
  section_url,
  COUNT(*) AS rows_per_url,
  COUNT(DISTINCT contents) AS distinct_contents,
  COUNT(DISTINCT length(contents)) AS distinct_lengths
FROM read_parquet('RPGnet--roleplay-by-post-play-forum--part1.parquet')
GROUP BY section_url
ORDER BY rows_per_url DESC
LIMIT 20;
```

For `page-88` (100 rows) and `page-76` (100 rows), both `distinct_contents` and `distinct_lengths` equaled 100. This tells us that **within those sampled page groups, no exact string duplicates were present**. It does **not** prove uniqueness across the entire dataset or across the two files, because:
- Only ~45 distinct `section_url` values were observed in the Part 1 sample.
- The check was limited to a few URLs, not all 4,466 / 4,997 rows.
- Exact string equality is stricter than byte-for-byte equality if whitespace or attribute ordering differs.

**Stage 1 - Exact dedup (executed):**

```sql
CREATE OR REPLACE VIEW both_parts AS
SELECT * FROM read_parquet('RPGnet--roleplay-by-post-play-forum--part1.parquet')
UNION ALL
SELECT * FROM read_parquet('RPGnet--roleplay-by-post-play-forum--part2.parquet');

SELECT
  md5(contents) AS content_hash,
  COUNT(*) AS occurrences,
  COUNT(DISTINCT section_url) AS distinct_section_urls
FROM both_parts
GROUP BY 1
HAVING COUNT(*) > 1
ORDER BY occurrences DESC;
```

**Result:** 0 exact duplicates across both files.

**Verification query:**
```sql
SELECT 
  COUNT(*) AS total_rows,
```

2. Should thread listing pages and thread detail pages be ingested as separate entity types, or should all content be normalized to posts only?
3. How should OOC vs IC distinction be captured when it is only present in title text and post content?
4. What is the desired handling of very large rows (up to 33 MB) - full thread dumps vs. individual posts?
5. Should edit history (textual "Last edited:" markers) be preserved as structured data, or is the current post text sufficient?
6. How should duplicate or near-duplicate content across Part 1 and Part 2 be deduplicated, if at all?
7. What is the appropriate granularity for the CDM: forum → thread → post, or should pages also be entities?
8. How should quote attribution ("Username said:") be normalized - as blockquotes with attribution, or as a separate reference entity?

## Decisions for Ingestion Design

1. **Error and non-forum pages**
   - Skip error pages and other non-forum-content pages.
   - They are not CDM content and do not require recovery at ingestion time.

2. **CDM granularity**
   - A forum thread becomes a CDM `Document`.
   - A forum post becomes a CDM `NarrativeItem` within that `Document`.
   - Forum/source information remains source metadata.
   - Listing pages and other forum structural pages are not ingested as CDM entities; their information may be used to derive relationships where useful.

3. **Thread / post identity**
   - The thread is the asynchronous session represented by the `Document`.
   - Posts are the constituent items of that document and retain their source identity.
   - The `Document` is reconstructed as the union of its posts rather than treated as an independently captured page snapshot.

4. **OOC / IC material**
   - Where OOC material can be distinguished from the narrative, preserve it as `reasoning` on the relevant `ContentVariant` rather than discarding it.
   - This preserves the human coordination/context surrounding the collaborative narrative and leaves room for future `refine-cdm-reasoning` processing.

5. **Large documents**
   - Large source rows are not, by themselves, a reason to split a thread.
   - CDM `Document` is capable of representing a complete literary work, so thread size should be constrained only by an actual processing or storage limitation encountered in practice.

6. **Edited posts**
   - Ingestion represents the current/latest available post content.
   - Historical edit versions are not preserved as separate content at this stage.

7. **Duplicate posts**
   - Exact duplicate source rows will be identified mechanically across the complete acquired corpus.
   - Where the same post identity occurs more than once, retain the latest version by source timestamp.
   - The post identity, rather than the containing page snapshot, is the unit of deduplication.

8. **Duplicate threads**
   - A duplicated thread must not result in duplicated CDM `Document` records.
   - Thread identity is used to locate the corresponding CDM `Document`, while its constituent posts are unioned into that document.
   - A later or reacquired source containing additional posts therefore contributes to the existing thread rather than creating another document.

9. **Ingestion idempotency / restart model**
   - RPGnet ingestion will first structure the acquired source into threads and posts, deduplicating posts by identity.
   - It will then use the `LedgerIndex` to locate the existing CDM `Document` for each thread and reconstruct/union the document from its posts.
   - This permits the source to be reacquired and ingested again without requiring a special-case recovery path.
   - The same pattern should remain applicable to other asynchronous-session corpus sources.

10. **Source structure**
    - The source repository layout is retained under the acquisition root as provided by the source:
      `lemonilia/roleplaying-forums-raw/`.
    - RPGnet is a source within that catalog, not an additional directory level invented by LilaKosha.

11. **Deduplication verification**
    - A full-corpus exact-content duplicate scan was performed as a deterministic ingestion pre-check.
    - The scan found 0 exact duplicate rows across the 9,463 acquired rows.
    - Near-duplicate detection beyond exact deduplication is **not pursued** at this stage.
    - The thread/post identity and union model is the primary mechanism for protecting CDM integrity.
